petravanta

Personal growth apps

Practice tools that plan a session, coach you through it and track how you improve. Everything runs in the browser and keeps your data on your device.

  • Fitness Coach

    Plans gym workouts that fit your time, equipment and sports, runs them set by set with a rest timer, and tracks strength by exercise and movement.

    Open →Source

  • Skating Mentor

    Plans skating practice for your skates and spot, tracks confidence in each skill, and maps what each skill unlocks next.

    Open →Source

  • VoiceTrainer

    Live resonance, vowel, sibilant and pitch feedback for voice training, with guided drills and reading practice.

    Open →Source

  • Nutrition Helper

    Logs food against daily budgets for calories, protein, fat, carbs and sodium, and tracks weigh-ins and body fat toward a goal weight.

    Open →Source

  • Special Trip Planner

    Plans a surgery, a long hike, a festival or a conference from a template written by people who have done it, with the why behind every rule.

    Open →Source

  • Photo Timeline

    Lines your photos up by the date they were taken on one or more tracks, marks trips and special days, and saves the timeline as an image.

    Open →Source

  • Project Tracker

    Plans a project as a Gantt chart with linked tasks that move when the work before them does, and tracks how much is done against the plan.

    Open →Source

  • Study Buddy

    Prepares you for flash-card style knowledge tests like the U.S. citizenship civics test, with practice tests you set up and a history of every answer.

    Open →Source

  • Mind Palace

    Mind maps you arrange by dragging ideas around, with an outline of the same map beside it that you can edit, reorder and indent.

    Open →Source
